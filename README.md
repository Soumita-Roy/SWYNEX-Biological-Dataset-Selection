# SWYNEX Internship - Task 1: Biological Dataset Selection

## 🦠 Chosen Dataset
* **Name:** SARS-CoV-2 Complete Genome Sequences (Delta vs. Omicron Variant Alignment)
* **Primary Repository:** NCBI Virus Database / GenBank
* **Accession Link:** [NCBI Virus SARS-CoV-2 Data](https://nih.gov)

## 📊 Dataset Metadata & Size
* **Data Type:** Fasta nucleotide sequence files (.fasta) representing complete viral genomes and associated epidemiological metadata.
* **File Size:** ~30 MB (For a curated alignment subset of 1,000 high-coverage genomic sequences).
* **Data Dimensions:** 1,000 complete genome sequences, each approximately 29,903 base pairs (nucleotides) in length.

## 🔬 Proposed Research Question
> *"What specific non-synonymous mutations have accumulated in the Spike (S) protein gene when comparing the Delta variant to the Omicron variant of SARS-CoV-2, and how do these genomic variations correlate with their evolutionary transmission timeline?"*

## 💡 Scientific Significance
Tracking mutations in the Spike (S) protein is critical because this specific region allows the virus to bind to human ACE2 receptors. By identifying and aligning the genetic sequence differences between the Delta and Omicron variants, researchers can pinpoint mutations responsible for increased transmissibility or immune evasion. This genomic surveillance data is vital for adapting diagnostics, updating mRNA vaccines, and preparing for future viral lineages.
